# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Now available for Minecraft 1.21 to 1.21.11 and 26.1 to 26.3 on Fabric and NeoForge.
- One file per loader covers 1.21 and 1.21.1, another covers 1.21.2 to 1.21.11, and another covers 26.1 to 26.3.
- Fixed the hedge recipe not loading on 1.21.1, so hedges can be crafted again.
- Hedges can be composted.
- Flowering hedges are now shaded like plain hedges instead of looking brighter than them.
- New eyeblossom hedges (1.21.4 and later) that open at night and close during the day like real eyeblossoms, and golden dandelion hedges (26.1 and later).
- Flowering hedges now break faster with a hoe, like plain hedges.
- Picking a flowering hedge (middle click) now gives a hedge.
- Shears from other mods can snip flowers off hedges too.
- The Fabric version now needs Fabric API.
